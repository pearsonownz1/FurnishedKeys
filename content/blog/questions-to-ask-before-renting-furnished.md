A few good questions up front can save you a surprise at move-in. Send these in your first message, or save them for a quick call.

## Cost
1. **What's the total monthly cost?** Ask for rent plus utilities, so you know the real number.
2. **How much is the deposit, and when is it returned?**
3. **Are there one-time fees,** such as for cleaning, pets or parking?
4. **How is rent paid, and when is it due?**

## Dates
5. **What's the minimum stay?**
6. **Can I extend if I need to, and how much notice do you need?**
7. **What happens if I need to leave early?**

## The place
8. **What's included?** Ask about linens, kitchen supplies, laundry and how fast the Wi-Fi is.
9. **Where do I park, and is there storage** for bikes, skis or gear?
10. **Is anyone else on the property?** Ask whether the owner lives in the main house and whether any spaces are shared.

## Rules and move-out
11. **What are the house rules** on pets, smoking, guests and quiet hours?
12. **What's expected at move-out?** Ask about the cleaning standard, key return and checkout time.

Get the answers in writing, through Furnished Keys messages or in your rental agreement.
