If you're planning a stay of a few months in Central Oregon, here's what renters are paying right now and how a furnished place compares.

## Bend
- **$2,519 a month** is the average rent across all bedroom counts and property types, according to [Zillow Rental Manager](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/bend-or/) (updated October 4, 2026).
- **$2,300 a month** is the median, according to [Zumper](https://www.zumper.com/rent-research/bend-or) (October 2026). Zumper puts the average one-bedroom apartment at $1,695 and the average two-bedroom at $1,815.

The two sources differ because they track different listings and calculate differently. Use them as a range, not as an exact number.

## Redmond and Sisters
Zillow's typical rent index for July 2026, as reported by [The Move Genie](https://themovegenie.com/oregon/sisters/cost-of-living), was $2,098 a month in Redmond and $2,059 in Sisters, compared with $2,302 in Bend. Sisters is small, so there are few rentals there, and the cheaper ones go quickly.

## How furnished monthly rentals compare
The figures above are mostly for standard, unfurnished long-term rentals. A furnished monthly place usually costs more, because the rent covers furniture, housewares and often utilities and Wi-Fi, and because the owner takes on more turnover. How much more depends on the place and the season, so compare actual listings.

## Budget for the full cost
When you compare a furnished place with an unfurnished lease, count what you won't have to buy or set up yourself:
- furniture, a bed, kitchenware and linens, or a moving truck,
- utility and internet setup and deposits,
- the commitment of a 12-month lease.

Every Furnished Keys listing shows the rent, utilities, the deposit and any fees separately, so you can compare the full monthly cost.

*These figures are from the third-party sources linked above as of October 2026. Rents change month to month.*
