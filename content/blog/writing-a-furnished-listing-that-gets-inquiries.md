Renters choose a furnished place on three things: how it looks, what it really costs, and whether it's available when they need it. A good listing answers all three before anyone has to ask.

## Photos come first
- Shoot in daylight with the lights on and the blinds open.
- Photograph every room, plus the entrance, the parking and any outdoor space.
- Pick a cover photo that shows the main living space.
- Upload at least five photos. Listings with fewer feel unfinished.

## Write a specific title
"Cozy ADU" doesn't help anyone. Try something like "Private 1BR ADU with Washer/Dryer, 5 Min to Old Mill." Name the type, the size, a standout feature and the location.

## Description: who it's for and what living there is like
Open with two sentences on who the place suits: a solo professional, a couple, or a family. Then cover:
- the sleeping arrangements,
- the kitchen, the workspace and the Wi-Fi speed,
- laundry, parking and storage,
- what's nearby,
- whether you live on the property.

## Spell out every cost
List the rent, utilities, the deposit and any fees separately. Surprise costs are the top reason renters drop off, and a clear breakdown builds trust.

## Keep availability current
Confirm your dates regularly. Renters see when you last confirmed availability, and a recent date tells them you're responsive.

## Reply quickly
The first owner to reply often gets the renter. Answer inquiries from your dashboard within a day.
