If you've taken a contract at St. Charles in Bend, you need a place that's ready on day one, that matches the length of your contract, and that doesn't add a long drive to a 12-hour shift. Here's how to find one.

## Match the rental to your contract
Most travel contracts run for weeks or a few months, which is longer than a vacation rental and shorter than a lease. Look for listings with a minimum stay at or below your contract length, and ask the owner about extending, since contracts often get renewed.

## Stay close to the hospital
St. Charles Bend is on the east side of town. Staying on the east side or near the Parkway keeps your commute short, especially after night shifts and on winter mornings. If you'll work at more than one location, check the drive to each.

## What matters on a shift schedule
- **Blackout curtains,** if you work nights
- **Quiet hours.** Ask whether anyone else lives on the property.
- **Laundry in the unit** for scrubs
- **Parking** close to the door
- **A real kitchen** for meal prep
- **Flexible check-in** for when you arrive after a long drive

## Questions to ask the owner
- Can my start date move if my contract does?
- What happens if my contract is canceled early?
- Are utilities included, or billed on top of rent?
- Is there a monthly rate, or a discount for a longer stay?

See our [full list of questions to ask](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-renting-furnished).

## Inquire early
Send your dates as soon as you have an offer. Mention that you're a travel healthcare worker and how long your contract runs. Owners appreciate knowing the stay is tied to a set end date.
