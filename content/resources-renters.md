# Renter resources

Moving somewhere for a few months is different from signing a yearlong lease. These guides cover what to look for, what to ask, and how to plan a stay.

## Start here
- [How to find a furnished monthly rental in Bend](/blog/finding-a-furnished-monthly-rental-in-bend)
- [What "furnished" should include: a move-in checklist](/blog/what-furnished-should-include)
- [Questions to ask before you rent a furnished place](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-renting-furnished)

## Before you inquire
- **Know your dates.** Owners reply faster when you give a move-in date, a move-out date, and how flexible you are.
- **Add up the full cost.** Each listing separates rent from utilities, deposits and other fees. Add them together before you compare places.
- **Say who's coming.** Tell the owner how many people and pets will be staying.
- **Check the minimum stay.** It's listed on every property page.

## Staying safe
- Talk with owners through Furnished Keys until you've seen the place, in person or on a video tour.
- Never wire money or pay with gift cards.
- If a deal seems too good to be true or someone pressures you to pay right away, email hello@furnishedkeys.com.
