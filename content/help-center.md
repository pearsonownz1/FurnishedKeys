# Help center

## Your account
- **I can't sign in.** Use "Forgot password" on the [sign-in page](/sign-in). The reset email comes from noreply@furnishedkeys.com. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
- **How do I change my email or phone number?** Open your dashboard, go to Settings, and click Save. You'll see "Saved" when it's done.

## For owners
- **How do I add a listing?** Sign in to your dashboard and add a listing from Listings. You can save a draft and come back to finish it later. Drafts appear under Listings.
- **Why isn't my listing live yet?** Every listing is reviewed before it's published. If anything is missing, we'll email you to say what to change.
- **How do I block dates?** Open Calendar in your dashboard and select the dates you want to block. Renters will see them as unavailable.
- **What does "Confirm availability" do?** It marks your dates as current, so renters can see when you last checked them.
- **How do I reply to a renter?** Open Inquiries, pick the conversation, and write your reply. You can also decline an inquiry.

## For renters
- **How do I contact an owner?** Open a listing and click "Send inquiry." Include your dates, who's coming, and any pets.
- **I haven't heard back.** Owners reply on their own schedule. If a few days pass, inquire about another place too.

## Contact us
Email hello@furnishedkeys.com and we'll get back to you.
