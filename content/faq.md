# Frequently asked questions

## For renters

**What is Furnished Keys?**
Furnished Keys helps you find a furnished home for a monthly or extended stay. We're starting in Central Oregon, with Bend and Deschutes County, and expanding across the Northwest.

**Who rents through Furnished Keys?**
Mostly traveling professionals, families relocating to the area, contract workers, and people between homes who need a furnished place for a few months rather than a year.

**How long can I stay?**
Each listing sets its own minimum stay, and it's shown on the listing page. Most places on Furnished Keys are for stays of a month or longer.

**Does it cost anything to inquire?**
No. Sending an inquiry is free. Your message goes straight to the owner or manager of the listing.

**What's included in the rent?**
Every listing shows the monthly rent apart from utilities, deposits and any other fees, so you can see the full cost before you reach out. If something isn't clear, ask the owner in your inquiry.

**Can I book instantly?**
Not yet. For now you send an inquiry, the owner replies, and the two of you agree on dates and terms.

**Are pets allowed?**
That depends on the listing. Filter for pet-friendly places when you browse, and mention your pets in your inquiry.

**How current is the availability?**
Each listing shows when the owner last confirmed its availability.

## For owners

**How much does it cost to list?**
Nothing. Listing is free. Furnished Keys only earns when your place actually rents.

**Who decides who stays?**
You do. Renters send inquiries to you, and you choose who stays and when.

**What kinds of places can I list?**
Furnished ADUs, apartments, rooms and whole houses that are available for monthly or extended stays.

**Can I manage more than one listing?**
Yes. Your owner dashboard holds all of your listings, inquiries and calendars in one place.

**Does Furnished Keys offer full management?**
Ask us about it. Request a free rental assessment and we'll talk through your options.

**Will my property show up without my permission?**
No. A place is only published when its owner submits it and approves it.

**Do I need a permit to rent monthly?**
The rules depend on your city and county. Check with your local planning office before you list.
